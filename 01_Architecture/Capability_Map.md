# Capability Map

**Phase:** 0.2 — System Definition  
**Version:** 1.0

## Govern

- manage policies, ownership, approvals, and exceptions;
- record architectural decisions and system versions;
- classify risk and enforce lifecycle rules.

## Know

- capture canonical knowledge as versioned Markdown;
- structure documents with metadata and relationships;
- retrieve relevant context with provenance;
- detect stale, conflicting, or missing documentation.

## Plan and Coordinate

- translate goals into bounded tasks and workflows;
- assign work to humans, agents, or automations;
- manage dependencies, approvals, status, and handoffs.

## Execute with AI

- define specialized AI worker roles;
- provide scoped context, instructions, memory, and tools;
- require approval at consequential decision points;
- evaluate quality, safety, reliability, and cost.

## Integrate Tools

- register and classify approved tools;
- manage credentials and permission scopes;
- invoke external services through controlled adapters;
- monitor availability, limits, failures, and spend.

## Manage Data

- define canonical entities and relationships;
- validate, store, retrieve, and migrate operational data;
- apply privacy, retention, lineage, and access rules;
- support event history and auditability.

## Automate Operations

- run scheduled, event-driven, and human-triggered workflows;
- coordinate retries, idempotency, timeouts, and recovery;
- surface failures and request intervention when needed.

## Deliver Applications

- provide clear interfaces for creators, operators, and administrators;
- expose shared capabilities through stable service contracts;
- maintain product-specific boundaries without duplicating the core.

## Protect and Verify

- authenticate identities and authorize actions;
- protect secrets and sensitive data;
- log material events and support incident response;
- test code, prompts, agents, workflows, integrations, and releases.

## Measurement

Each capability will later receive an owner, maturity level, service expectation, risk classification, and measurable acceptance criteria.
