# Creator OS Foundry System Charter

**Phase:** 0.2 — System Definition  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Baseline

## Purpose

Creator OS Foundry is the governed foundation for designing, operating, and evolving an AI-enabled creator operating system. It brings documentation, AI workers, tools, data, automations, applications, security, and quality controls into one coherent platform.

## Mission

Enable creators and operators to turn strategy into repeatable execution through trustworthy AI assistance, reusable system knowledge, controlled integrations, and observable workflows.

## Outcomes

The Foundry will:

- preserve institutional knowledge as versioned Markdown;
- coordinate human and AI work through explicit roles and approvals;
- expose approved tools through a governed registry;
- maintain durable operational data with clear ownership;
- automate repeatable work while keeping consequential decisions reviewable;
- support multiple applications without duplicating core logic;
- make security, testing, and traceability part of every system layer.

## Governing Principles

1. **Human authority remains explicit.** AI may recommend and execute within approved boundaries; accountable humans retain final authority for consequential actions.
2. **Documentation is part of the system.** Decisions, interfaces, procedures, and controls are versioned alongside implementation.
3. **Least privilege by default.** Every user, agent, tool, and automation receives only the access required for its purpose.
4. **Composable before bespoke.** Shared capabilities are designed once and reused across workflows and applications.
5. **Observable and reversible.** Material actions produce logs, status, ownership, and a recovery path.
6. **Secure by design.** Secrets, private data, and external writes are controlled at system boundaries.
7. **Evidence over intuition.** Decisions and releases rely on defined acceptance criteria and verifiable results.

## Phase 0.2 Exit Criteria

Phase 0.2 is complete when the system purpose, scope, boundaries, capabilities, domain concepts, ownership model, and non-functional requirements are documented, internally consistent, and approved as the baseline for detailed design.
